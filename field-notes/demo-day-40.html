FIELD NOTE

The demo always works. Ask what happens on day 40.

Kathy·2 min read·November 2026

Have you ever chosen software in a week, then spent the next 2 months working around it?

Most growing businesses go through this at least once. Someone sits through a demo of Airtable, monday.com, Smartsheet or a smaller ERP like Odoo. The sample data is clean, the board fills itself in, and it looks like the end of the spreadsheet. 2 weeks in, everyone's enthusiastic. By day 40 there's a new spreadsheet sitting next to the new tool.

“A demo shows you the process the vendor expected. Day 40 shows you the one you actually run.”

The part I pay attention to

Day 40 is roughly when the first real exception turns up. A customer splits an order across 2 deliveries. A supplier sends half a shipment. Someone needs sign-off from a manager who isn't on the system. None of that was in the demo, so the team handles it the old way, with a message, a note or a side file. The tool quietly becomes one more place to update.

WHAT'S ACTUALLY HAPPENING
The tool handles the normal case well
The exceptions go back to chat and spreadsheets
Someone keeps both in sync by hand

What I'd do before choosing a tool

Write down your 3 most annoying exceptions from last month, the ones that caused a phone call. Then ask every vendor, including us, to show those 3 live, with your data. Not the happy path. Watch for the moment the screen stops and someone says “you could use an integration for that.”

The tool that handles your exceptions without a side file is usually the right one, even if its demo was less impressive. Sometimes that's a project board. Sometimes it's a connected system built on something like AnyDB. Sometimes it's the spreadsheet you already have, with 2 fixes.

Judge a tool by your worst week, not its best demo.

Where AI fits

AI demos are the same, only faster. An app AI builds in an afternoon always works on day 1. Ask what it does with your worst exception on day 40, who can see and undo what it changed, and who maintains it next year.
