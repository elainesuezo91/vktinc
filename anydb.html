What is AnyDB?

A business database for operations and workflows.

AnyDB is a platform for operational systems that don't fit neatly into a packaged software category — the work that usually ends up in spreadsheets, or bolted onto an ERP or CRM that was never designed for it. You model the real things in your business as connected records, then run the process on top of them.

Records & relationships

Model items, projects, assets, vendors, claims — and how they connect.

Views & forms

Each team sees what it needs; structured capture replaces email requests.

Workflow automation

Routing, approvals, reminders and handoffs as work progresses.

Dashboards & reporting

Live operational data instead of a monthly export.

Portals & permissions

Controlled access for customers, vendors and partners.

APIs, cloud or on-prem

Connects to the systems you already run; deploy where you need to.
