What we keep seeing

Growth doesn't break systems all at once.

A spreadsheet that worked for five people becomes risky at thirty. Information fragments across tools. Reporting gets harder. People invent workarounds, and eventually the systems stop reflecting how the business actually operates.

None of that means the business did anything wrong, and it rarely justifies a transformation programme. It usually means one or two workflows have outgrown the way they're held together.

Spreadsheets aren't the problem. The problem is what everyone has to do around them.
