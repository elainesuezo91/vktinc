Three good first jobs

Routine work AI can take on, and the decision it leaves with you.

Most businesses have at least one of these running through a spreadsheet and an inbox today.

01 · MONTH-END Matching the bank lines

AI does: matches transactions against the books and lists the differences.

A person decides: what each difference is, and when the month is closed.

02 · QUOTE → CASH Approved quote to invoice

AI does: spots approved quotes that haven't been invoiced, and drafts the invoice.

A person decides: whether the invoice goes out.

03 · APPROVALS Chasing the approval

AI does: routes the request, reminds the approver, and records the answer.

A person decides: yes or no.
